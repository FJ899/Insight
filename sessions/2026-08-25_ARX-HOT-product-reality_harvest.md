## 1. WHAT CHANGED

The program moved from primarily asking which ecosystem components survive a stronger-model counterfactual to a second, independent product question:

> How much Human operational intelligence is the current system actually removing while preserving Human semantic authority?

This produced HOT-001 as a frozen AS-IS observational baseline, separate from ARX-001.

A second continuity problem was then identified: valuable AI reasoning and conceptual breakthroughs can remain trapped in chat history even when technical state/TODO are preserved.
